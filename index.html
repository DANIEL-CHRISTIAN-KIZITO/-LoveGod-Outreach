Who We Are

Born to Give Back

LoveGod Outreach Team is a youth-led, faith-driven movement that turns birthdays into community blessings — one month at a time.

We are a team of young Christians from Uganda who believe that every birthday is more than a personal celebration. It is a God-given opportunity to pour love into the communities around us.

Founded on a simple but powerful conviction, we organize structured community outreach programs where members who share the same birth month come together, identify a vulnerable community, and serve them with practical support and the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

We are not waiting to grow up to make a difference. We are the church today — and today, we choose to give.

✝️

🤝

How It Works

The Birth Month Model

Our mode of operation is personal, sustainable, and beautifully simple. Here's how every outreach is born.

01
🎂

Group by Birth Month

Members who share the same birth month form a dedicated sub-team for that month's outreach.

02
📍

Choose a Community

The sub-team identifies a vulnerable community — hospitals, schools, rural areas, or urban slums.

03
🙌

Mobilize Resources

The team gathers support internally and through partners to fund and equip the outreach.

04
❤

Serve & Minister

On outreach day, practical items are distributed and the Gospel of Jesus Christ is shared.

05
📖

Report & Give Thanks

The team documents impact, shares testimonies, and prepares for the next month's team.

The 12-Month Cycle

Every Month, Someone Gives

Our calendar of compassion — 12 months, 12 opportunities to change a life. July is where we begin.

Inaugural Outreach — July 2026

Kayunga Hospital:
Caring for Expectant Mothers

📍 Kayunga District Hospital, Uganda

Reaching Pregnant Women in Their Most Vulnerable Season

Our first outreach targets expectant mothers in the maternity ward of Kayunga District Hospital — women who need practical support, dignity, and the hope of the Gospel as they prepare to bring new life into the world.

Mid-July 2026 Kayunga Region Maternal Support Gospel Ministry

What We Will Provide

  • Sanitary pads & personal hygiene kits
  • Baby clothes, diapers & wrapping cloths
  • Nutritional food packages (porridge, rice, oil)
  • Soap and washing basins
  • Small towels and bedding items
  • Scripture cards and Gospel tracts
  • Prayer and personal ministry

"Above all else, we go to bring the hope of Jesus Christ — because a mother who meets the Saviour receives far more than a gift bag. She receives life."
