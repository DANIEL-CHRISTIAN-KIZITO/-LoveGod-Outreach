Where it began

Kayunga was more than a visit.

In our first outreach, LoveGod Outreach Team went to Kayunga Referral Hospital with one simple conviction: people deserve to be met with dignity, kindness, and the hope of Christ.

We were grateful to serve together with Jesus Saves Street Ministry, led by Evangelist Timothy, who guided us during the mission in Kayunga.

How it works

One team for every birth month.

LoveGod Outreach Team operates through 12 teams. Members who share a birth month form a team, plan together, and reach their communities through one outreach each month. Each team has a WhatsApp group for prayer, planning, and coordination.

Mission in review

Otuboi was a testimony of faith in action.

LoveGod Outreach Team joined Geuza Ministries, led by Pastor Andrew, in Otuboi, Kalaki District. Through prayer, worship, and Gospel ministry, more than 280 souls accepted Jesus as Lord and Saviour.

A mission of impact

Over 280 people made a commitment to follow Jesus as Lord and Saviour during the outreach.

A mission of partnership

We are grateful to Geuza Ministries and Pastor Andrew for leading and serving with us in Kalaki District.

A mission of gratitude

To every minister, volunteer, prayer partner, and supporter: thank you for helping make this mission possible.

Meet the founder

Faith with sleeves rolled up.

Minister Daniel Christian

LoveGod Outreach Team was founded by Minister Daniel Christian in response to God’s love: to mobilize young people, serve communities, and make the Gospel tangible through action.

From the first step in Kayunga to the EOS Revival Mission, the vision is clear: love God deeply, love people practically, and keep showing up.
