Where it began

Kayunga was more than a visit.

In our first outreach, LoveGod Outreach Team went to Kayunga Referral Hospital with a simple conviction: people deserve to be met with dignity, kindness, and the hope of Christ.

We were grateful to serve together with Jesus Saves Street Ministry, led by Evangelist Timothy, who led us during the mission to Kayunga.

Meet the founder

Faith with its sleeves rolled up.

Minister Daniel Christian

LoveGod Outreach Team was founded by Minister Daniel Christian as a response to God's love: to mobilize young people, serve communities, and make the Gospel tangible through action.

From the first step in Kayunga to the EOS Revival Mission, the vision is clear: love God deeply, love people practically, and keep showing up.
